# One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge

One line roasting my phone:

> My phone heats up the second I open the camera, like it's blushing at the thought of being seen with me.

## Why it works

The joke starts on a detail every phone owner recognises — the handset going warm the moment the camera opens — then reframes the heat as embarrassment. The double meaning does the work: a phone that overheats is also a phone too shy to be seen with its owner, and the sting lands on the last three words, where the roast turns back on me. One sentence, no setup to explain, and it reads cleanly out loud.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (and `./entry.txt` for local verification).
